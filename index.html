Why this exists

Pollinations serves text, image and audio models from many labs through one key. Asking two labs the same question is cheap; asking six at once and reading the answers next to each other is the only way to see which parts of an answer are the question's fault and which are the model's. Nothing is cached, hidden or aggregated into a single "consensus" number: the answers stay separate, and the split panel only groups what already sits on the screen.

Every call spends the reader's own Pollen through Bring your own Pollen. No key is sent anywhere except gen.pollinations.ai, and it stays in this tab's session storage.
